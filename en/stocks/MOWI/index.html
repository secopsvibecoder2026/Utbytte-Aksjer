About the company

Mowi is the world's largest producer of Atlantic salmon and runs fish farming in Norway, Scotland, Ireland, the Faroe Islands, Iceland, Canada and Chile. The Icelandic part is Arctic Fish in the Westfjords, where Mowi owns the majority and which has its own share listing on Euronext Growth Oslo. The name goes back to Mowi, the fish farming company the Mowinckel family started in the 1960s. The group was long called Marine Harvest and took the old name back from 1 January 2019.

What sets Mowi apart from the other salmon stocks is how much of the chain it owns itself. Mowi Feed has two feed plants, one in Norway and one in Scotland. After a strategic review, the board decided in December 2025 to keep them and instead make the feed to Skretting's recipes, cooperating on purchasing and logistics. At the other end of the chain the group has value-added processing in Europe, the United States and Asia and sells smoked salmon, portions and ready meals, partly under the MOWI brand. In Northern Norway Mowi has grown through the purchase of Nova Sea in 2025 and an agreement from March 2026 to take over the sea farming business of Torghatten Aqua.

The dividend goes ex four times a year. The policy is that the ordinary quarterly dividend shall under normal conditions be at least half of underlying earnings per share, and that capital the company does not need shall be paid out as an extraordinary dividend. The amount therefore follows earnings from quarter to quarter, and a weak quarter quickly shows up in the next payout. The group reports in euros, while the dividend is declared in Norwegian kroner, so the krone exchange rate also affects what is left for shareholders.

Tax for investors outside Norway

Mowi is a Norwegian company, and Norwegian companies deduct 25% withholding tax on dividends to shareholders resident abroad. If a tax treaty between Norway and your country gives a lower rate, you can get it when the dividend is paid by giving your custodian or account operator the documentation, or apply to the Norwegian Tax Administration for a refund afterwards, within five years. Shareholders resident in the EEA may instead use the risk-free return allowance where that gives less tax, and companies resident in the EEA may be exempt. Your own country may also tax the dividend.
