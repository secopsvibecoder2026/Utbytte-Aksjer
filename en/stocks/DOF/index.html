About the company

DOF Group owns and operates vessels that supply oil and gas fields, handle anchors and carry out work on the seabed. The company also sells complete subsea projects, with its own engineers and remotely operated vehicles on board. The shipping company was founded in Austevoll in 1981 to take part in the new offshore industry in the North Sea, and many of the vessels still carry Skandi names. Today the fleet works across large parts of the world: in the North Sea, in North America and Guyana, in Asia and Australia, and not least in Brazil. There Petrobras has many of the vessels on long contracts, and in May 2026 DOF won twelve-year contracts for four new vessels to be built at a Brazilian yard.

The company on the stock exchange today is not the same as the old DOF. After the years of overcapacity in the offshore fleet, the shipping company could not get out from under its debt, and in March 2023 the creditors took over the whole group. The old shareholders, with the Møgster family as the largest, were left with shares in a company that no longer owned any of the operations. In 2024 DOF bought Maersk Supply Service from A.P. Møller Holding, paying partly in new shares, so the Danish owners became a large shareholder. The share trades under the ticker DOFG.

The dividend came only once the debt had been refinanced in 2025, and it is now paid every quarter. It is set in dollars and paid in kroner. The company has said that cash beyond the debt level it is aiming for will be returned to shareholders. How much that comes to depends on how many vessels are working, and on the construction of the four new vessels, which under the plan are largely to be financed with local loans in Brazil.

Tax for investors outside Norway

DOF is a Norwegian company, and Norwegian companies deduct 25% withholding tax on dividends to shareholders resident abroad. If a tax treaty between Norway and your country gives a lower rate, you can get it when the dividend is paid by giving your custodian or account operator the documentation, or apply to the Norwegian Tax Administration for a refund afterwards, within five years. Shareholders resident in the EEA may instead use the risk-free return allowance where that gives less tax, and companies resident in the EEA may be exempt. Your own country may also tax the dividend.
