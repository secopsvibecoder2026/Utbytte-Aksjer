About the company

CMBTO is the Oslo listing of the Belgian shipping company CMB.TECH, which has its head office in Antwerp and also trades in Brussels and New York under CMBT. The company was called Euronav and owned only crude oil tankers until 2024. That year it bought CMB.TECH Enterprises from its main shareholder CMB, bringing dry bulk carriers, container ships, chemical tankers, offshore wind vessels, port vessels and the hydrogen business into the group, and it took the name CMB.TECH in October the same year. In 2025 the company first bought Hemen's stake in Golden Ocean and then merged the dry bulk owner into itself. The merger was completed on 20 August 2025, and the shares were listed on Oslo Børs the same day, so Golden Ocean shareholders in Oslo received new shares there.

The largest owner, with a majority of the shares, is CMB, which the Saverys family controls through Saverco. CEO Alexander Saverys and CFO Ludovic Saverys are both co-owners. By far most of the revenue comes from the ships, and since Golden Ocean dry bulk weighs heavily alongside the tankers. The hydrogen arm is building production of green hydrogen and ammonia and developing engines for such fuels, and it is still in a build-up phase. In 2026 the company has sold a number of VLCCs and Suezmax ships at a gain, and such sales create one-off items in the results.

Since the end of 2023 the board has had full discretion over distributions, with no fixed share of earnings. A distribution can also be made up of two parts, as in June 2026: an ordinary dividend and a repayment from the share premium account. Belgium deducts withholding tax from the dividend part, but not from the repayment. The amounts are declared in dollars. Shareholders in Oslo receive kroner, a few days after the money has been paid out in Brussels and New York.
